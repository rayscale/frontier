Frontier Sans is a variable sans-serif with a tall x-height, round bowls, and flat, deliberately cut terminals. It is the typeface of the design language and it is built from source in the repository.

It is original work. It is not derived from Geist, Outfit, Inter, SF, or any other typeface.

<Callout title="Version 0.1" tone="warning">
  Frontier Sans has not been reviewed by a type designer. Its kerning is derived by a script, and
  some symbols, such as `@`, `&`, `§`, and `¶`, are still rough.
</Callout>

## Weights

One axis, `wght`, runs from 300 to 700 with Regular at 400.
